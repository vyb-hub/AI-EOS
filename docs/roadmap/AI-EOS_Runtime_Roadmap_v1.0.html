Long-Term Architecture

The Founder never talks to a model directly

Every step is mediated by the Runtime. Models are interchangeable behind Roles; the chain below is not.

Founder → AI-EOS Runtime → Workflow Engine → Stage Router → Role Engine → Provider SDK → Underlying AI Providers

Two Domains

Product OS, then Engineering OS

No implementation occurs in Product OS. Engineering begins only after Product Readiness Review.

Domain 01
Product OS

Transforms an idea into an engineering-ready product.

  • Founder Discovery
  • Market Discovery
  • Product Strategy
  • Business Strategy
  • Validation
  • Product Definition
  • UX Discovery
  • Product Architecture
  • Product Readiness Review — gate

Engineering OS
