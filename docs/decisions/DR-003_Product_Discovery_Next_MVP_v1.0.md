# Decision Record DR-003

**Title:** Product Discovery Is the Next AI-EOS MVP
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 July 2026
**Status:** Approved by Founder
**Decision source:** Founder instruction in the canonical strategic workspace

## Context

EC-003 proved that AI-EOS can execute an approved Engineering Charter through
research, architecture, documentation alignment, implementation, review,
verification, approval gates, and knowledge capture.

The Runtime still begins at the engineering boundary. It cannot yet transform
a raw idea into an evidence-backed, Founder-approved product definition and
Engineering Charter.

## Decision

The next MVP priority is **Product Discovery**.

Product Discovery will prove that AI-EOS can accept a raw Founder idea and
produce an evidence-backed, decision-ready MVP definition that can enter the
existing Engineering Runtime.

## Intended Product Discovery Flow

```text
Raw Founder Idea
→ Founder Discovery
→ Problem and Customer Definition
→ Market and Competitor Research
→ Opportunity and Risk Assessment
→ Product Strategy
→ MVP Scope and Prioritization
→ Product and UX Specification
→ Product Readiness Review
→ Founder Approval
→ Engineering Charter Handoff
```

## Required Outputs

- Founder intent record
- Problem statement
- Target customer and use cases
- Market and competitor findings with sources
- Assumptions, risks, and validation gaps
- Product strategy
- Prioritized MVP scope and explicit non-goals
- Product requirements and acceptance framing
- Initial UX or journey specification
- Product Readiness Review evidence
- Founder approval record
- Draft Engineering Charter for the existing Runtime

## Guardrails

- AI must not invent Founder vision, values, or priorities.
- Market claims require attributable evidence.
- Research findings must distinguish evidence from inference.
- Product scope does not advance without explicit Founder approval.
- Product Discovery performs no implementation.
- Engineering begins only after Product Readiness Approval and an approved
  Engineering Charter.
- Source-control and publication actions remain separately authorized.

## Sequencing

1. Test and evaluate the completed Engineering Runtime MVP.
2. Capture defects and lessons from testing.
3. Draft and approve the Product Discovery MVP charter and architecture.
4. Implement and validate Product Discovery.
5. Run a governed idea-to-Engineering-Charter pilot.
6. Connect the approved handoff to the existing Engineering Runtime.

## Current Authority

This decision sets priority. It does not itself authorize Product Discovery
implementation. A new Engineering Charter is required.
