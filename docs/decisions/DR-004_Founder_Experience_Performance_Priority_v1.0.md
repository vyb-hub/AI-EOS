# Decision Record DR-004

**Title:** Founder Experience and Time-to-Value Priority  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 31 July 2026  
**Status:** Founder-approved  
**Applies to:** EC-004 Product Discovery MVP

## Decision

AI-EOS Product Discovery must feel like a fast Chief of Staff rather than expose
the Founder to every internal workflow stage.

Founder experience, time-to-first-value, visible progress, and interruption
reduction are the top corrective priority before EC-004 completion.

## Service Targets

- visible acknowledgement: under 2 seconds
- useful initial brief: under 15 seconds
- Fast result: under 60 seconds
- Standard evidence report: target 3–5 minutes
- no more than two planned Founder decision interruptions
- every active view explains progress, current learning, and required input

Targets are product objectives measured in controlled validation, not guarantees
for every provider or network condition.

## Product Changes

1. Default to Fast mode.
2. Support Fast, Standard, and Deep research budgets.
3. Produce and persist an immediate Idea Brief.
4. Stream meaningful progress while work continues.
5. Combine approval decisions where governance permits.
6. Revise only affected evidence instead of repeating whole research stages.
7. Maintain one living HTML Product Brief.
8. Make waiting optional.
9. Use an independent challenger before consequential recommendations.
10. Use an LLM Council only for high-impact, disputed, or explicitly requested
    decisions.

## Council Policy

The Council is not the default research engine. It is an escalation mechanism
after primary research and independent challenge.

Eligible decisions include target-market selection, final MVP selection,
material investment, and decisions with unresolved high-impact disagreement.

## Current Run

The custom-packaging Product Discovery run remains paused at MVP Scope Approval.
Its completed Founder Intent and revised Research Evidence remain valid.

## Authority

Founder approved this direction on 31 July 2026 with `LFG`.
