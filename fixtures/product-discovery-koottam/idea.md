# The Koottam — Kakkanad Community Wellness & Culture Space

Kakkanad works hard. This is a proposal for a dedicated local space where it
can breathe.

The Koottam would be Kakkanad's first dedicated space for movement, art,
authentic food, and community — designed for IT professionals, families, and
culture-seekers who live in the area but currently have nowhere to truly
unwind.

## Concept

A small, real-estate-light community center built around:

- An open-air pavilion (800–1,000 sq ft) for dance, Kalaripayattu, and events
- A small, aesthetic cafe / chill space with pergola seating
- A compact office + toilet block
- Natural landscaping and pathways connecting the spaces

## Target audience

IT professionals, families, and culture-seekers living in and around
Kakkanad who want a nearby place for movement, art, food, and community —
not a gym, not a mall, not a restaurant, but a "third place" to unwind.

## Realistic build option (~₹20L)

A cost-conscious, Kerala-climate-friendly version of the concept, designed
to be simple, aesthetic, functional, and buildable in phases:

- Open pavilion (800–1,000 sq ft): ₹8.5L
- Toilets + office block: ₹3.5L
- Cafe / pergola seating: ₹2.5L
- Flooring (red oxide + brick): ₹1.5L
- Landscaping + pathways: ₹1.5L
- Lighting + electricals: ₹1.5L
- Plumbing + septic: ₹1.0L
- **Total (approx.): ₹20L**

Design priorities: natural cooling (trees + open sides), low maintenance
(red oxide + brick), cross ventilation (high roof + open plan), warm/low-energy
lighting, and a design that is scalable — buildable in phases rather than
all at once.

## What we don't yet know

This is a founder concept, not a validated plan. Market size, pricing,
willingness to pay, competing venues in Kakkanad, and the right sequencing
of phases are all open questions this discovery process should help answer.
